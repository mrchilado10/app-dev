# Transformers

## Overview
Transformers is a science fiction action film directed by Michael Bay. It tells the story of two alien robot races — the Autobots and the Decepticons — who bring their war to Earth.

## Main Characters
- **Optimus Prime** – Leader of the Autobots  
- **Bumblebee** – Brave and loyal scout  
- **Megatron** – Ruthless leader of the Decepticons  

## Why I Like It
> I love Transformers because of its amazing visual effects, exciting battle scenes, and strong themes of teamwork and courage.

## Favorite Quote
*"Freedom is the right of all sentient beings."* – Optimus Prime
